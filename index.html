Bio

Ioannis Galbos / Stockholm, Sweden

Tracks released by LAUS MUSIC - London, Juan's Kitchen Delights - Skopje and 2 Rhythm 4 Record - UK.

Ioannis Galbos is a Stockholm-based electronic music producer and DJ, originally from Skopje. Starting his musical journey around 2005, he has spent nearly two decades shaping dancefloor-focused electronic music. His productions have been released on respected imprints including LAUS MUSIC (London), Juan’s Kitchen Delights (Skopje), and 2 Rhythm 4 Record (UK). Drawing influence from artists like Lawrence and Tim Paris, his sound is defined by its deep and refined layers. He has closely collaborated with the electronic music organisation Loomynum Collective, delivering standout DJ mixes that resonated strongly across their platform. In addition to his studio work, he has performed at curated underground events for dedicated audiences.
